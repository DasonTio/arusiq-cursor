01 · What is being builtD6 §1 · D3

One hidden gadget, three dashboards, four outbound channels

A small module inside the air conditioner turns any brand into a monitored asset. Everything else — the alerts, the bill, the carbon, the service call — is derived from what it reports.

02 · What is monitoredUR-MNT-02…06 · FR-20, 23, 24

Twelve parts, three groups, one severity each

“Monitor every aspect” is not a slogan in the requirements — it is an enumerated list. Each part carries its own health, its own evidence, and its own recommended action.

Indoor unit

5 parts
  • Air filterpressure drop, run hours
  • Evaporator coilapproach temp, frost
  • Blower motor & fancurrent, vibration
  • Condensate drain & panlevel, overflow
  • Vents & louversairflow, actuator

Outdoor unit

4 parts
  • Condenser coildischarge vs ambient
  • Compressorcurrent, cycling, vibration
  • Condenser fan & bladescurrent, imbalance
  • Refrigerant linessuperheat, subcooling trend

Electrical & control

3 parts
  • Thermostat & sensorsdrift, disagreement
  • Capacitor & contactorstart current, chatter
  • Electrical wiringvoltage sag, harmonics

Acute signals — act before it stops

Vibration outside the learned envelope, discharge temperature rising against ambient, and a fall in superheat or subcooling that means charge is being lost. Each names the part it implicates and the measurement that triggered it. FR-23

Slow signals — trend, not threshold

A gradual charge decline consistent with a microscopic leak; a rising pressure drop consistent with filter or coil fouling. Reported as a trend with a projected failure date and a confidence band, so a cheap repair replaces an emergency one. FR-24

05 · The most sensitive featureEP-09 · UR-PAY-01…06 · FR-50…57

Cooling is never cut off. It is stepped down, with a gate at every step

The interview asked for the ability to slow or stop an air conditioner when a rent-to-own customer stops paying. Taken literally that is a switch. Specified responsibly it is a ladder — and the R2 revision added the half that was missing: how service comes back, and how a customer argues.

06 · The commercial claimFR-61, 70, 71, 72 · UR-ENE-07

“What it would have been” is a number we have to defend

Every savings figure is a comparison against a counterfactual. The method behind that counterfactual is published, versioned and dated inside the product — and the chart is not permitted without a link to it.

CO₂ ppm is not kgCO₂e

Indoor carbon-dioxide concentration tells you the air is stale and you will feel sleepy. Greenhouse-gas emissions from electricity are a different quantity in a different unit. They are never shown on the same card, and never added together. UR-AIR-06 · FR-66

Avoided emissions are not carbon credits

Until an accepted methodology and an independent verifier say otherwise, a saving is a saving. The prototype never uses the word “credit”; the tokenization screen is a labelled mock; and Scope 2 reporting is what gets sold first. UR-CAR-08

07 · SequenceD6 §5 · §12

Clickable first, hardware last

The interview was explicit about the order: prove the experience before building the thing that feeds it. Each phase is a real gate, not a label on a plan.

09 · Still openD8 §6

Five things the revision does not fix

These are honest limits, not oversights. They are listed here so the design set is signed off with them in view rather than discovered during the pilot.

No legal basis yet

Payment-linked restriction needs a contract clause and a per-market review. Phase 1A demonstrates a labelled simulation; enforcement waits for sign-off.

A split unit recirculates air

Fresh-air control needs a connected fan, fresh-air unit or damper. Where none exists the interface must say so and give the manual action.

No registry route for credits

AC-efficiency micro-offsets have no accepted methodology confirmed. Sell Scope 2 reporting first; never print the word “credit”.

Voice in Bahasa Indonesia

Recognition accuracy is unproven. Because text is equally capable by requirement, poor recognition degrades convenience, not capability.

Two decisions to settle before the sprint starts

Who may flag a space as health-sensitive — it blocks the stop step of the ladder — and whether one or two approvers are required at each step. Both are cheap to decide now and expensive to retrofit; both change FR-52 and FR-53.
